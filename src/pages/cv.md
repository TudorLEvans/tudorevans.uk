# Tudor Evans CV

I consider myself an all-rounder and enjoy working cross-functionally with product, marketing and sales. I believe in an output-focussed approach to management, and like working with high talent individuals who can be given ownership and responsibility over their own problem spaces. Some highlights of my career:

- Achieving 10% month on month growth in the Speechmatics self-service revenue
- Building a massively scalable usage and billing system for ASR telemetry
- Leading a high-performing team trusted by the business to execute on multiple complex engineering challenges

As well as people management and tech, I love working as a communicator. I have received very positive feedback about my presenting and writing styles, and enjoy public speaking.

In my current role at Speechmatics, I'm the team lead for our self-service product offering. Check it out at [portal.speechmatics.com](https://portal.speechmatics.com).
